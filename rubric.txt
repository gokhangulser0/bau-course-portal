Problem 4/72: Magnitude of the Force Acting on the Pin at D

TOTAL: 100 POINTS

1. REFERENCE SOLUTION (FOR THE GRADER)

------------------------------------------------------------------

Given:

  - Applied horizontal load: $P = 100\text{ N}$ to the right at point $B$.

  - Triangular plate pinned to fixed support at $A$.

  - Pin $C$ is fixed on vertical member $DE$ and moves in a smooth slot cut into the triangular plate.

  - Member $DE$ is pinned to fixed support at $D$ and supported by a smooth roller against a vertical wall at $E$.

  - Dimensions:

    - Vertical distance from $A$ to $B$: $h_{AB} = 140\text{ mm} = 0.14\text{ m}$

    - Horizontal distance from $A$ to member $DE$ (and pin $D$): $x_D = 160\text{ mm} = 0.16\text{ m}$

    - Vertical height of pin $C$ above level of $A$ and $D$: $y_C = 120\text{ mm} = 0.12\text{ m}$

    - Vertical height from pin $C$ to roller $E$: $h_{CE} = 80\text{ mm} = 0.08\text{ m}$

    - Total height of member $DE$ ($D$ to $E$): $h_{DE} = 120\text{ mm} + 80\text{ mm} = 200\text{ mm} = 0.20\text{ m}$



Step 1 - Geometry of the Slot and Force Direction at Pin $C$:

  - Distance from $A$ to $C$:

    $$d_{C/A} = \sqrt{(160\text{ mm})^2 + (120\text{ mm})^2} = \sqrt{25600 + 14400} = \sqrt{40000} = 200\text{ mm} = 0.20\text{ m}$$

  - The slot line forms a 3-4-5 right triangle with slope angle:

    $$\theta = \arctan\left(\frac{120}{160}\right) = \arctan(0.75) = 36.87^\circ$$

  - Because the slot is smooth, the interaction force $F_C$ is normal to the slot axis.

  - Moment arm of $F_C$ about pin $A$ is simply the length $d_{C/A} = 200\text{ mm}$ because $F_C$ is perpendicular to line $AC$.



Step 2 - Equilibrium of Triangular Plate ($\sum M_A = 0$):

  - Taking moments about pin $A$ (counterclockwise positive):

    $$\sum M_A = 0 \implies (100\text{ N})(140\text{ mm}) - F_C (200\text{ mm}) = 0$$

    $$14,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{mm} - 200 F_C = 0 \implies F_C = \frac{14000}{200} = 70\text{ N}$$

  - The slot exerts a normal force $F_C = 70\text{ N}$ on pin $C$, directed down and to the right at an angle of $36.87^\circ$ to the vertical ($53.13^\circ$ to the horizontal).



Step 3 - Vector Components of Force $F_C$ on Member $DE$:

  - Horizontal component:

    $$F_{C,x} = F_C \cos 53.13^\circ = 70 \times \left(\frac{120}{200}\right) = 70 \times 0.6 = 42\text{ N} \quad \text{(to the right)}$$

  - Vertical component:

    $$F_{C,y} = F_C \sin 53.13^\circ = 70 \times \left(\frac{160}{200}\right) = 70 \times 0.8 = 56\text{ N} \quad \text{(downward)}$$



Step 4 - Equilibrium of Member $DE$:

  - Roller at $E$ exerts a horizontal reaction $E_x$ to the left (vertical component $E_y = 0$).

  - Taking moments about pin $D$ ($\sum M_D = 0$, counterclockwise positive):

    $$\sum M_D = 0 \implies F_{C,x}(120\text{ mm}) - E_x(200\text{ mm}) = 0$$

    $$(42\text{ N})(120\text{ mm}) - 200 E_x = 0$$

    $$5040 - 200 E_x = 0 \implies E_x = \frac{5040}{200} = 25.2\text{ N} \quad \text{(to the left)}$$



  - Horizontal force equilibrium ($\sum F_x = 0$):

    $$D_x + F_{C,x} - E_x = 0 \implies D_x + 42 - 25.2 = 0 \implies D_x = -16.8\text{ N}$$

    Thus, $D_x = 16.8\text{ N}$ (to the left).



  - Vertical force equilibrium ($\sum F_y = 0$):

    $$D_y - F_{C,y} = 0 \implies D_y - 56 = 0 \implies D_y = 56\text{ N} \quad \text{(upward)}$$



Step 5 - Magnitude of Force at Pin $D$:

  $$D = \sqrt{D_x^2 + D_y^2} = \sqrt{(-16.8\text{ N})^2 + (56\text{ N})^2} = \sqrt{282.24 + 3136} = \sqrt{3418.24} \approx 58.47\text{ N}$$



Final Answer:

  $$D = 58.5\text{ N} \quad \text{(or } 58.47\text{ N)}$$

3. GRADING POLICY (LENIENT - THIS IS A QUIZ)

------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Error carried forward (ECF): If an early calculation mistake is made 

   (e.g., incorrect moment arm for $100\text{ N}$ force or miscalculating $F_C$), 

   do NOT penalize it again in later steps. Give full credit for Part C and D if 

   the student's subsequent work is mathematically consistent with their earlier $F_C$ value.



2. Tolerance: Accept final magnitude values within $\pm 3\%$ of reference value 

   ($D = 56.7\text{ N} - 60.2\text{ N}$).



3. Unit slips: Mixing $\text{mm}$ and $\text{m}$ consistently (e.g., keeping all distances in 

   $\text{mm}$) is completely acceptable. Deduction for unit confusion costs at most 

   3 points total.



4. Alternative methods: If a student uses vector cross-products ($\vec{r} \times \vec{F}$), 

   instantaneous centers, or global frame equilibrium, award full credit for 

   equivalent milestones when mathematically correct.



5. Minor arithmetic errors: A single arithmetic error with correct formulas loses 

   at most half of that line item's points.



6. Correct method, wrong or missing number: Award at least 50% of that line's points.



7. Incomplete solution: Grade completed sections normally. Do not penalize missing 

   later parts beyond their own point allocations.



8. Partial work: Give partial credit for any correct, relevant equilibrium equation 

   or geometric calculation.



9. Presentation: Do not deduct for handwriting quality unless unreadable.



10. Blank or irrelevant answer: 0 points. Any genuine attempt with a valid FBD or setup 

    should receive 15 points or more.

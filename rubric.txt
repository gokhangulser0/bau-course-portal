1. REFERENCE SOLUTION (FOR THE GRADER)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Given: Beam length = 1.2 + 1.8 + 1.8 = 4.8 m (O at x = 0, A at
1.2 m, B at 3.0 m, C at 4.8 m). The beam is fixed at O (cantilever),
so the reactions are O_x, O_y and a moment M_O.

Loads:
  - Weight: W = m*g = 500 * 9.81 = 4905 N = 4.905 kN, acting
            downward at the beam's midpoint, x = 2.4 m.
  - 1.4 kN acts upward at A (x = 1.2 m).
  - 15 kN*m couple at B, counterclockwise.
  - 3 kN at C, 30 deg from the vertical, pointing down and to the
    left:
        3 sin30 = 1.5 kN   (in -x direction)
        3 cos30 = 2.598 kN (in -y direction)

Sum of forces in x:
  O_x - 1.5 = 0
  O_x = 1.5 kN  (to the right, +x)

Sum of forces in y:
  O_y + 1.4 - 4.905 - 2.598 = 0
  O_y = 6.10 kN  (upward, +y)

Sum of moments about O (counterclockwise positive):
  M_O + 1.4(1.2) - 4.905(2.4) + 15 - 2.598(4.8) = 0
  M_O + 1.68 - 11.772 + 15 - 12.471 = 0
  M_O = 7.56 kN*m  (counterclockwise)

  (The 1.5 kN x-component at C acts along the beam axis and
   produces no moment about O. The 15 kN*m couple is a free
   moment and is NOT multiplied by any distance.)

Final answer:
  O_x = 1.5 kN,  O_y = 6.10 kN,  M_O = 7.56 kN*m (CCW)
  (Resultant reaction force |O| = approx. 6.28 kN - optional)

3. GRADING POLICY (LENIENT - THIS IS A QUIZ)
------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Error carried forward (ECF): If an early mistake is propagated
    consistently, do NOT penalize it again in later parts. Give
    full credit for later steps that are correct given the
    student's earlier values.

2.  Tolerance: Accept answers within +/-3% of the reference values
    (e.g., O_y = 5.92-6.28 kN, M_O = 7.33-7.79 kN*m).

3.  Value of g: g = 9.81, 9.8, or 10 m/s^2 are all acceptable.
    Do not penalize the small differences this causes in the
    final numbers.

4.  Unit slips: Mixing N and kN, or N*m and kN*m, costs at most
    3 points in total, as long as the numerical work is
    consistent.

5.  Sign / direction convention: If the student clearly states a
    different but reasonable sign convention (e.g., clockwise
    positive), grade against the student's own convention. Do not
    deduct more than 3 points in total for sign or direction
    errors alone.

6.  Alternative methods: Taking moments about another point (such
    as C) and then using force equilibrium is fully acceptable.
    Treating the distributed weight as a single force at the
    midpoint is expected.

7.  Minor arithmetic errors: A single arithmetic slip with correct
    method loses at most half of that line's points.

8.  Correct method, wrong or missing number: Award at least 50% of
    that line's points.

9.  Missing weight: If the student forgets the beam's weight but
    otherwise uses a correct method, deduct only the points of
    A2 and do not penalize the later numerical values (ECF).

10. Partial work: Give credit for any correct, relevant step even
    if the student did not finish.

11. Presentation: Do not deduct for messy handwriting or a
    missing diagram beyond the 8 points in A1, or for unit
    formatting beyond the 15 points in Part D.

12. Blank or irrelevant answer: 0 points. Any genuine attempt with
    at least a correct setup should receive 15 points or more.

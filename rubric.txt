Problem 3-83: Pumped-Storage Revenue (Pump-Turbine System)
TOTAL: 100 POINTS

1. REFERENCE SOLUTION (FOR THE GRADER)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Given: Height h = 40 m, volume flow rate = 2 m^3/s in either
direction, pump-motor efficiency = 75%, turbine-generator
efficiency = 75%, 10 h/day in each mode, 365 days/year.
Electricity price: $0.05/kWh (buying at night), $0.12/kWh
(selling during the day). Frictional losses in piping are
neglected.

Step 1 - Mass flow rate:
  m_dot = rho * V_dot = (1000 kg/m^3)(2 m^3/s) = 2000 kg/s

Step 2 - Ideal (reversible) power for lifting/dropping the water:
  W_ideal = m_dot * g * h = (2000)(9.81)(40) = 784,800 W
          = 784.8 kW

Step 3 - Pump mode (night, electricity is bought):
  Electric power input = W_ideal / eta_pump
                       = 784.8 / 0.75 = 1046.4 kW
  Energy per day  = 1046.4 kW * 10 h = 10,464 kWh
  Cost per day    = 10,464 * 0.05 = $523.2
  Cost per year   = 523.2 * 365 = approx. $190,970

Step 4 - Turbine mode (day, electricity is sold):
  Electric power output = W_ideal * eta_turbine
                        = 784.8 * 0.75 = 588.6 kW
  Energy per day  = 588.6 kW * 10 h = 5,886 kWh
  Revenue per day = 5,886 * 0.12 = $706.3
  Revenue per year = 706.3 * 365 = approx. $257,800

Step 5 - Net annual revenue:
  Net = 257,800 - 190,970 = approx. $66,800 per year

Final answer:
  Pump input = 1046 kW, turbine output = 589 kW,
  net potential revenue = approx. $66,800 per year.

3. GRADING POLICY (LENIENT - THIS IS A QUIZ, LONG PROBLEM)
------------------------------------------------------------------
1.  Error carried forward (ECF): If an early mistake is
    propagated consistently, do NOT penalize it again in later
    parts. Give full credit for later steps that are correct
    given the student's earlier values.

2.  Tolerance: Accept answers within +/-5% of the reference
    values (e.g., net revenue = $63,500-$70,200 per year,
    pump input = 994-1099 kW, turbine output = 559-618 kW).
    Because the net revenue is a difference of two large
    numbers, small rounding differences can shift it noticeably;
    do not penalize rounding.

3.  Values of g and rho: g = 9.81, 9.8, or 10 m/s^2 and
    rho = 1000 or 997 kg/m^3 are all acceptable. Do not penalize
    the differences these cause in the final numbers.

4.  Unit slips: Mixing W and kW, kWh and Wh, or MW and kW costs
    at most 4 points in total, as long as the numerical work is
    consistent.

5.  Efficiency applied in the wrong sense: If the student
    multiplies by 0.75 for the pump (instead of dividing), or
    divides for the turbine, deduct A3 only and apply ECF to the
    later numbers. Give full credit for B2/C1 if they are
    consistent with the student's own (wrong) treatment.

6.  Efficiency used only once or not used: Deduct A3 only and
    apply ECF to the later numbers.

7.  Gross revenue reported as the final answer: If the student
    correctly finds the turbine revenue (approx. $257,800) but
    never computes the pumping cost, give full credit for
    Parts A and C, and award 8 of the 20 points for D1. If the
    student computes both but does not subtract them, award
    14 of the 20 points for D1.

8.  Days per year: Using 360 or 365 days is acceptable. If the
    student only gives the DAILY net ($183) and does not convert
    to a year, award 12 of the 20 points for D1.

9.  Alternative methods: Computing energy directly (e.g., energy
    per day instead of power first) or working in different
    units is fully acceptable if the result is consistent.

10. Minor arithmetic errors: A single arithmetic slip with
    correct method loses at most half of that line's points.

11. Correct method, wrong or missing number: Award at least 50%
    of that line's points.

12. Partial work: Give credit for any correct, relevant step
    even if the student did not finish.

13. Presentation: Do not deduct for messy handwriting or for
    unit formatting beyond the 10 points in D2.

14. Blank or irrelevant answer: 0 points. Any genuine attempt
    with at least a correct idea of the setup should receive
    20 points or more.
